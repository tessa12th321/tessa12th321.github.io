✨ What Is shipstores?

shipstores is a small helper program that sits between two things you already use: your AI assistant and the app stores. In technical circles this kind of helper is called an MCP server, but you can think of it as a bridge or an adapter. One side speaks to your AI agent, and the other side speaks to the places where mobile apps actually get published.

That means your AI agent can do the boring parts of a release for you. It can look up your app, check what version is already live, prepare a new submission, and push your build to the right store. You describe what you want in plain language, and shipstores turns that into the exact series of actions the stores expect.

It connects to three destinations: App Store Connect for iOS, Google Play Console for Android, and Expo EAS for teams that build with Expo. It works alongside tools like Claude and Claude Code, and it replaces the old ritual of hand-writing release scripts or keeping a long checklist taped to your monitor.

A release request fails. Read the message your agent shows you. Store messages are written for humans, and they almost always say exactly which detail is missing.
